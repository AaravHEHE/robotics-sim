    Build from VEX parts
    
      
    Snap C-channel, wheels, motors, gears and more together on VEX's ½″ hole grid. The build becomes the robot's 3D model: it is only how the robot looks. How it drives and what it can do still come from its profile (the blue box is its size).
    
      
    
        
    
          
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
            
            
            
          
    
          
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
        
        
        
      
    
    
    
      

  
    
    
      
    Field model
    
      
    Load the official field CAD: the ZIP from VEX's field CAD page (e.g. v5rc-override-fieldcad.zip), or a GLB / OBJ export of it. Converting the full STEP field takes a few minutes the first time; after that it loads in a second. It stays in this browser: it is never uploaded or published. It only changes the look; the simulation still uses the measured field. Pins, Cups, Toggles and small hardware in the model are left out (the simulation draws the game pieces where they really are), and a converted STEP is colored by part number (other models keep their own colors).
