| Every lift type rated (`M-VIMP61KZQ`) | 2-bar/4-bar: arc, 1–2 layers. 6-bar: higher, mostly level, still an arc. DR4B: straight up, level, tall and stable, heavy. It must go up and over stacks, which limits height, and needs precise alignment for upright stacks and Loaders. |

CPSLO: flex-wheel stage + big roller + polycarbonate funnel that assembles the Cup over the Pin, counter-roller, Toggle roller. |
