# Override robot presets

The seven Override presets in `data/robots/`: one per lift type (**DR4B, DR6B, 2-bar, cascade, continuous, 4-bar,
6-bar**), each drawn from a real Onshape CAD (see [onshape-import.md](onshape-import.md)). The earlier presets
(Banshee, ACE, Toggle control bot, Midfield pusher) were retired from the app; their profiles stay in
`tests/fixtures/robots/` to test the roller-claw, lobster-claw and toggle mechanisms. The DR6B and continuous presets reuse
the DR4B and cascade physics (their lift kinematics are the same family), with 12 in links and four stages. Each
preset below was modelled on a real design. Each one is modelled on a real design (sources in
[reference/videos.md](reference/videos.md)), has a mechanism test (`samples/test-*`, generated by
`scripts/gen-mechanism-tests.cjs`) and its own 15 s auton. Picking a robot in the app opens its
auton; the **Test** button opens its mechanism test.

To plan a route for a robot, run `node scripts/plan-reach.ts <robot-id>`. It prints where the claw
is (robot frame) at each lift setting, and the height a held stack's bottom must reach to go onto
an alliance Goal holding 0–4 Pins. A claw sets a stack down when it is within 1.5" of the Goal's
center, with the stack's bottom 2" below to 4" above its resting height, and the claw tilted less
than 20°.

All distances are inches. "Ahead" is the robot's +y; the robot center is the turning center.

## DR4B + intake chamber, 8059A-style: `override-dr4b`

15 × 15 × 15, 6-motor traction drive at 360 rpm.

| Mechanism | Port | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | 10, blue | 3-stage, 600 rpm. Carries pieces over the robot into the chamber (450 ms). |
| DR4B | 7 + 8, red, 12:84 | Standoff-linkage DR4B at the back. The chamber is 11" behind the center and rises straight up (z 2 at 0°, about 34 at 110°). |
| Chamber | ADI A | Piston clamp; the Preload starts in it. Open, it receives from the intake while the DR4B is down. |
| Toggler | — | Passive bumper. Hit at more than 40 in/s, it turns a Toggle two faces. |
| GPS | 12 | 6" behind the center, facing backward (the robot heading is its yaw + 180°). |
| Chamber eye | 9, optical | Looks down into the chamber: its proximity rises when a piece arrives. |

**Piece path.** Floor stack or Loader → intake → chamber → clamp → DR4B up → back into the Goal.
**Heights.** DR4B 13°: a Pin over an empty Goal. 25°: over 1 Pin. 45.5°: over 2 Pins. The samples
start it 3" off the wall (−57.6, −37): at the preset spot the Preload in the chamber would be in the wall.
**Auton** (`override-dr4b`, LemLib + GPS, Red 1 south): backs the Preload into R1, intakes two
stacks and backs each onto it (a 3-Pin stack), then hits the Red 1 Toggle at speed. 55, AWP.
**Skills** (`override-skills`, 60 s): the same 3-Pin stack and Toggle, then a loaded stack from the
south red Loader onto R2 (a short intake burst takes exactly one), the Red 2 Toggle, and a Midfield
park. 48 points.

## Cascade + chain-bar claw (Claw Gate physics, Banshee CAD): `override-cascade`

No reveal of the real Claw Gate / Clawscade was found; this combines 99182E and ACE. 15 × 15 × 18.

| Mechanism | Port | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cascade | 7 + 8, blue, 1:3 | 3 stages on a 1.375" spool: about 0.036" per spool degree. |
| Chain bar | 9, green, 1:3 | Rides the cascade carriage. 0° = straight out front (claw 11" ahead, 6" up); −25° = down in front of the intake (9.97 ahead, 1.35 up); 180° = out the back. The claw stays level. |
| Claw | ADI A | Piston claw; starts holding the Preload. |
| Intake | 10, blue | Funnel intake into the claw when the chain bar is down (hand-off at y 9.97, z 1.35). |
| Toggle bumper | — | Passive. |

**Heights** (chain bar at 0°, cascade spool degrees): 90 carries the Preload over an empty Goal, 70 a
Cup + Pin over 1 Pin, 270 over 2 Pins, 140 over the yellow Pin on a short neutral Goal.
**Auton** (`override-cascade`, LemLib, Red 1 south): drops the Preload into R1 and stacks an intaken
Cup + Pin on it, carries a second stack with the chain bar up while it rams the Red 1 Toggle, then
raises the cascade and sets the stack on neutral Goal N_R1. 55, AWP.

## 2-bar arm + wrist + roller claw (Banshee-style): `override-twobar`

A single arm (a 2-bar) like the Banshee robots at Override events (Joseph973's reveal): 15 x 15 x 14, 6-motor 450 rpm
drive, a flex-wheel intake (port 10) feeding a roller claw (port 9) on an 11 in arm (port 7, 1:5), with a 12:48 wrist (port
8) that turns the claw back by the arm's angle so it stays upright. It replaced the Flex Hero Bot physics, whose claw tilted
with the arm and could only set a Pin on an empty Goal. **Auton** (`override-twobar`, LemLib): a 3-Pin stack on R1 and the
Red 1 Toggle rammed to red, 55.

## 6-bar + wrist claw: `override-sixbar`

15 × 15 × 13. 6-bar (port 7, 1:5), piston claw (ADI A) on a motor wrist (port 8, 1:2) that can turn the
Preload over. The Preload starts yellow end down.
**Heights.** 6-bar 16°: the flipped Preload over an empty Goal. 27°: a Cup + Pin over 1 Pin.
**Auton** (`override-sixbar`, EZ-Template): turns the Preload red end down and drops it into R1,
turns the wrist back upright, clamps the stack at (−47, −47) and sets it on, then hits the Red 1
Toggle at speed after a run-up. 45.

## 4-bar + rear piston claw: `override-fourbar`

15 × 15 × 11. A 4-bar (port 7, 1:5) reaching out the back with a piston claw (ADI A), and a front
Toggle bumper.
**Heights.** 4-bar 18°: a Pin over an empty Goal (claw 12.35" behind). 36°: a Cup + Pin over 1 Pin.
Like the DR4B it starts 3" off the wall, so the Preload behind it is inside the field.
**Auton** (`override-workhorse-toggle`, EZ-Template): backs the Preload into R1, backs onto a
Cup + Pin and stacks it on, then presses the Red 1 Toggle. 45.

## How each robot picks pieces up

- **A robot with an intake** takes every piece through it: from the floor (a lying Pin is caught by whichever end
  reaches the intake's mouth first), from a standing stack, or from a Loader's bottom opening. The intake hands the piece
  to its claw (or chamber) when the claw is down and open at the hand-off. The claw sits inside the robot and does not
  reach the floor, a Loader or a Goal itself (`src/games/override/manipulators.ts`, `grab`).
- **A robot without an intake** clamps its claw around what is in front of it: a standing stack, a lying Pin, a loaded
  stack in a Loader's opening, or the pieces placed on a Goal.

## 60 s Skills autons

One per robot, each starting at Red 1 (south) on the Skills field: every Goal starts empty, and the Match Loads (3
red/yellow Pins, 4 blue/yellow Pins, 7 Cups) come in only through the red Loaders as loaded stacks (a Pin with a Cup over
it). Red halves score in red Quadrants, yellow halves where that Quadrant's Toggle is red, and every half on the center
Goal while the robot is in the Midfield (RSC3).

What scores most per trip: a Cup hides the half of the Pin it covers, so a loaded stack scores only its bottom half (5),
while a lone Pin (a lying cross Pin, or a yellow Pin pulled out of its Cup by a claw) set on an empty Goal or on top of a
stack scores both halves: 15 for a red/yellow Pin in a red Quadrant with its Toggle red, 20 for a yellow/yellow Pin, and on
the center Goal both halves while the robot is parked beside it.

| Sample | Robot | Score | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `override-skills` | DR4B | 93 | 3-Pin stack on R1, Red 1 Toggle, a lone Pin on N_R1, the red load on R2, 3 loads on the center Goal with a lone Pin on top, parked |
| `skills-dr6b` | DR6B | 93 | the same route on 12 in links (`barFor()` gives the bar angle for each height) |
| `skills-cascade` | Cascade | 93 | the same plan through the intake (the chain bar up at the Loader, down to hand each load to the claw) |
| `skills-continuous` | Continuous | 93 | the cascade route with the four-stage lift (3/4 of the spool angles) |
| `skills-twobar` | 2-bar | 78 | 3-Pin stack on R1, Red 1 Toggle, a lone Pin on N_R1, a load on R2, a lone Pin on the center Goal, parked |
| `skills-fourbar` | 4-bar | 78 | 2-Pin stack on R1, Red 1 Toggle, a load on R2 with the yellow Pin from the Cup at (-23.5, -23.5) on top, Red 2 Toggle, the yellow Pin from the Cup at (23.5, 23.5) on the center Goal, parked |
| `skills-sixbar` | 6-bar | 78 | the same plan with the front claw and wrist |

The 4-bar and 6-bar pull their claws in as they rise, so they can't reach the Cup on top of a load on the center Goal (the
robot would hit the Goal first); their extra points come from the yellow Pins in the two free-standing diagonal Cups, which a claw lifts out without moving the Cup (the wall Cups stand too close together to reach the middle one cleanly). The 4-bar and 6-bar routes use
LemLib rather than EZ-Template: EZ-Template's wheel odometry drifts whenever the robot touches a wall or a Cup, and these
routes run between Cups with an inch to spare. Each sample's golden score is in `tests/override-samples.test.ts`.

## What every auton has in common

- Starts by moving 2–3" away from the wall: turning in place right against it swings a corner into it
  (the rear-claw robots already start 3" out).
- Carries what it holds over a Goal, or over the Pins already on it, before moving onto it: held
  pieces are solid, so a stack carried too low hits the Goal or the stack instead of passing through.
- Folds its claw up (or out of the way) before pressing a wall Toggle with the bumper: a claw out
  in front, below the top of the wall, hits the wall first.
- Backs off before each turn near a Goal, and routes around Goals rather than past them
  (the golden tests fail on any Goal contact).
- Lets go of a Toggle after pressing it: a Toggle a robot still touches counts as neutral.
- No Match Loads in head-to-head autonomous (<SG11>); Skills uses the red Loaders.
